# Code of Conduct

Help keep this project welcoming and respectful. Be considerate of different
backgrounds and experience levels, offer constructive feedback, and focus on
the work rather than personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing
someone's private information without permission are not acceptable.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to
protect the community. For conduct concerns that can be shared publicly, open
an issue or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not disclose
private or sensitive information in a public issue.
